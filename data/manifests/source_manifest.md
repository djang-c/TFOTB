## Risks / open points
1. OMIM is the main legal risk: it is entangled via phenotype.hpoa, MONDO xrefs, ClinVar and Monarch/PrimeKG. Only xref IDs, no text; human to confirm that ID strings alone are acceptable.
2. HPO's custom licence forbids altering content/relationships; the commercial-use reading is disputed (primary quote silent, secondary says restricted); hpo.jax.org licence URL returns 404. Contact HPO / counsel before commercial launch; show version/date and acknowledgement in the demo.
3. ChEMBL CC BY-SA 3.0 share-alike applies to redistributed derivatives.
4. Orphadata AI-disclaimer clause applies to any LLM-assisted feature; Orphadata Products need a data-transfer agreement (not used).
5. PubMed abstract and PMC full-text copyright: do not publish bulk abstract or full text in the public repo; PMC bulk retrieval only via Cloud/OAI-PMH/E-utilities/BioC; legacy FTP removed per page.
6. Rate limits: E-utilities 3 requests/s without key vs 10/s with key (a key requires an NCBI account: human action); NIH RePORTER 1 request/s and off-peak for big jobs; HGNC, Reactome, ClinVar FTP, ClinicalTrials.gov, QuickGO, ChEMBL limits UNVERIFIED. Prefer file downloads over API loops.
7. Attribution/version display obligations to implement in the UI and README: HPO (version/date + acknowledgement), GO (release + DOI), ClinVar/NLM/ClinicalTrials.gov attribution, Orphanet citation, MONDO/ChEMBL/Reactome-graphics CC BY.
8. Third-party submitter rights in NCBI-hosted data (ClinVar, PubMed) - residual risk.
9. Fetch-tool limits: several official pages (ClinicalTrials.gov, OMIM, NCBI E-utilities help, PubMed README, hpo.jax.org, Orphadata terms) were not readable; a human should open them in a browser and replace UNVERIFIED cells.
10. Release numbers seen are as reported by the fetch tool's summary of GitHub/news pages (MONDO v2026-09-01, HPO v2026-09-01, Reactome V97, ChEMBL 37); re-check at connector build time and record file SHA-256.
11. No data-use agreement is required for the sources marked GO or GO-with-conditions as far as pages opened show; OMIM commercial and Orphadata Products need agreements and are out of scope.
