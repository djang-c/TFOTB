## (a) Positive route
1. **Best-supported: CLN3 disease <-> Niemann-Pick type C** (C05, C06, with C07/C08 for the CLN3 side; C14 as therapy-literature edge). One primary paper (eBioMedicine 2023) states the shared claim in its own words and tests it in human brain LE/Lys. Limits: N=5 JNCL, 3 NPC (lipids), cross-sectional post-mortem, single lab; BMP differs.
2. **Plausible but inferred: CLN7 disease <-> CLN3 disease via lysosomal chloride homeostasis** (C03 + C04). No single source links the two; the pairing is ours (juxtaposition), so it can only be a `hypothesis only` edge until an expert confirms.
3. **NPC <-> neuronopathic Gaucher** (C11, C12): review-level shared claim plus a mouse liver result. Weaker: no primary human CNS data seen; "neuronopathic" subtype not specifically addressed.
4. **Reusable assets**: DEM-CHILD (covers all NCL incl. CLN7 by record; C16) and INPDR for NPC (C19). The two assets cover different diseases of the pair, so a "reuse across diseases" story would be *DEM-CHILD for CLN3/CLN7* and *INPDR for NPC*; no single registry spans both. Not verified: whether either accepts outside patient groups or shares data on what terms.

CLN7 itself does **not** carry the positive route in what I could open; the route is CLN3 <-> NPC. Milasen identity (C02) is UNVERIFIED in my sources.
